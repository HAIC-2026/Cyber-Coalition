# Evidence Register

This register records candidate evidence and what it can support. The originating agency source is the authority for dataset facts. Datatrove and AI-generated summaries help discover and organize sources; they do not independently validate the underlying data. Confirm exact versions, fields, retrieval dates, and page references before submission.

## Open questions, not confirmed deficiencies

The current catalog review did not locate an authoritative, complete record for shelter capacity and current accessibility, up-to-date hurricane suitability and opening status, current county-to-state workflow, exercise corrective actions, mutual aid resource typing, or EOC activation records. Catalog absence is not proof that records do not exist. The project should use public/shareable records or synthetic demonstration data and label unavailable fields as unknown.

## Evidence record for each future claim

Record source title and owner, exact URL/item ID, source date and retrieval timestamp with timezone, native geography, fields used, query/filter, transformation, checksum or version when available, source-reported content, independent validation state, confidence, limitations, and the requirement or test informed. Separate source finding, inference, scenario assumption, proposed requirement, and agency question.


## Kapolei OHCA site/license reconciliation

The [State of Hawaiʻi StateGIS OHCA Facilities layer](https://geodata.hawaii.gov/arcgis/rest/services/HumanHealthSafety/MapServer/3) was queried on 2026-11-14 and spatially intersected with the 2020 Census Kapolei CDP boundary (GEOID 1530300). The result is 39 source records and 37 exact source-address plus six-decimal-coordinate keys. These are reconciliation keys, not a count of confirmed unique sites. A public-safe record and association crosswalk is in [`SITE_LICENSE_RECONCILIATION.md`](SITE_LICENSE_RECONCILIATION.md). It retains 27 organization-level records and excludes personal/home-linked rows from published detail. Co-located licenses and programs remain separate unless identity is supported; status=0 semantics and GIS capacity units are unresolved. The layer metadata attributes geocoding to OHCA Licensing Section API data dated 2026-09-15. This comparison does not verify current operations, license status, capacity, or emergency availability.

## Hawaiʻi tract inventory comparison

The 2020 Census TIGERweb statewide tract universe contains 461 GEOIDs. The candidate StateGIS Census and Coastal SVI sets each contain 435 unique GEOIDs and match one another exactly; 26 Census-only GEOIDs are absent from both, each with POP100=0 and HU100=0. GEOID 15003981200 (tract 9812) is one of the 26. The comparison concerns base geography only; hazard/resource joins and the 100% coverage target remain unverified.
